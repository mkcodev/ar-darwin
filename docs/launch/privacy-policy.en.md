Last updated: November 7, 2026

AR-Darwin ([NOMBRE]) is an app for tracing drawings with your phone's camera, made by mkcodev. This
policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: **the app does not collect, send
or share any personal data.**

## What the app does not do

- It has no accounts and no sign-in.
- It shows no ads and includes no advertising SDK.
- It uses no analytics, tracking or automatic crash reporting.
- It does not send your images, your drawings or any other data to its own servers or anyone else's.
- It does not sell or share data with anyone, because it has none.

## What is stored and where

Everything you create in the app is stored **only on your phone**, in the app's private storage:

- The images you import, downsized, with their thumbnails.
- Your projects: name, categories, how you split the image, which tiles you have finished, where the
  image sits on the paper and how long you have been drawing.
- Your settings: theme, language, drawing hand and other preferences.

The app sends it nowhere and mkcodev has no access to it. The only copy off your phone is Android's
own backup, if you turn it on (see "Android backup").

## Android backup

If Android backup is turned on, the system may include the app's data in the backup stored in your
Google account, as it does with other apps. That backup is made and managed by Google, not mkcodev:
the app plays no part in it and mkcodev cannot see it. It lets you get your projects back when you
switch phones or reinstall the app. It is covered by
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en) and you can turn it off or
delete it in your Google settings (Settings › Google › Backup).

## Permissions

The app only asks for the permissions it needs, and only when you use the feature that needs them.

- **Camera.** To show the image you are tracing over what the camera sees, and to take a photo you want
  to import. The camera image is processed on the spot, on your phone: it is never recorded or sent
  anywhere.
- **Photos and files.** To import an image, the app opens the system picker: you choose the image and
  the app only receives that one. It does not read the rest of your photo library or your files.
- **Save to your photo library** (if it ships at launch). When you finish a drawing you can save a photo
  of it to your photo library. The app only writes that photo; it does not read your library.
- **Receive shared images** (if it ships at launch). If you share an image with the app from another
  app, it receives it to import it. This needs no special permission.

You can revoke any permission at any time in your system settings. The app keeps working, except for
the feature that relies on that permission.

## Sending feedback

In Settings, "Send feedback" opens a GitHub page in your browser with a draft that includes the app
version, system and language. Nothing is sent unless you post it yourself. Whatever you post on GitHub
is covered by [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## How to delete your data

- To delete a project, remove it from the Library.
- To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear its data in your system settings.

- To delete the copy Android keeps, if backup is on, delete it in your Google settings
  (Settings › Google › Backup) or in your Google Drive account.

mkcodev keeps no copy, so it cannot recover anything you delete. If an Android backup exists, the
system may restore it when you reinstall the app.

## Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it does with your data (for example, if it adds accounts or sync), this
policy will be updated before the change reaches the app, and the date above will show it.
